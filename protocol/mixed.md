# Mixed session

Part of the coaching protocol — `AGENTS.md` is the entry point and its general rules
(retrieval loop, SRS rules, tracking) apply on top of this file. No warm-up — the gap sprint is the retrieval.

Requires an M or L budget (on S days it stays due — run a drill instead). Triggered automatically when `Consecutive reviews ≥ 3` in `PROGRESS.md`. Goal: break the review loop by combining a targeted gap sprint with new content — the learner always leaves with something genuinely new.

Structure (in this order — do not swap):

1. **Gap sprint:** pick the single most urgent open gap from the gap tracker (`Reviews since last seen` highest, or longest overdue SRS topic). Ask exactly **2 questions** on it, through the retrieval loop with full error analysis. Update gap tracker and SRS for those questions only. Do not run more than 2 questions — the point is to stay accountable to open gaps without getting stuck in review again.
2. **New topic introduction:** immediately after the gap sprint, move to the next topic in the curriculum and run `protocol/concept.md` steps 1–4 (prior knowledge, presentation, own example, contrast). Skip the connections step — that can happen in a dedicated follow-up. The concept file follows the normal concept output rules.
3. **Tracking:** reset `Consecutive reviews` to 0. Log the gap sprint and the new concept as a single session-log entry (`Type: mixed`).

The mixed session does not count as a full review for SRS purposes — only the 2 gap-sprint questions update SRS.
